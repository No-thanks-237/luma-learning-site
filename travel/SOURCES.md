# Globe resources

- earth-day.jpg: Three.js official examples texture, downloaded 2026-10-19 from https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg . Source repository: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures/planets . Used as Earth imagery, not a street-level map.
- land.geojson: Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons, downloaded 2026-10-19 from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson . Natural Earth data are public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Coastline points are generated from these coordinates at runtime.
- Gold particles, orbit, atmospheric shader and destination markers: Luma procedural scene. Eight fixed destinations are learning examples; markers are not a live comprehensive destination database.

The external Xingjian travel planner is embedded by URL, with no source redistribution, credential forwarding or claimed cross-origin itinerary synchronization.
