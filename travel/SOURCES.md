# Globe resources

- earth-day.jpg: Three.js official examples texture, downloaded 2026-10-19 from https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg . Source repository: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures/planets . Used as Earth imagery, not a street-level map.
- land.geojson: Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons, downloaded 2026-10-19 from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson . Natural Earth data are public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Coastline points are generated from these coordinates at runtime.
- Gold coast particles, ambient stardust and destination markers: Luma procedural decoration over the Cesium globe and its atmosphere. Eight fixed destinations are learning examples; markers are not a live comprehensive destination database.
- CesiumJS 1.146.0: vendored standalone browser build and official TypeScript declarations from the npm registry, verified against the package SHA-512 integrity. See cesium/UPSTREAM.json and cesium/LICENSE.md, ThirdParty.json, ThirdParty.extra.json. No default ion imagery, terrain or geocoder is enabled. The previous Three.js planet has been replaced by the Cesium map.
- Street imagery: https://tile.openstreetmap.org/{z}/{x}/{y}.png , © OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Tiles are fetched by the browser for the visible view only and are not redistributed in this repository. Usage policy: https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/ . No bulk download, prefetch, service-worker cache override or offline map feature. Keep browser Referer and normal cache headers. High-volume production should configure an appropriate hosted or self-hosted tile source.
- Learning landmark markers are hand-curated approximate coordinates and original exercise prompts, with a link to verify each location on OpenStreetMap. Arbitrary map points have coordinates and optional user-provided labels; no reverse geocoding or POI database is claimed. Globe imagery is a single global texture; street detail comes from the live OSM tiles, not higher-resolution satellite imagery.

The external Xingjian travel planner is embedded by URL, with no source redistribution, credential forwarding or claimed cross-origin itinerary synchronization.
